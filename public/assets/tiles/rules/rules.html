How to Play Bestagon Blvd

Grow your city's Population by balancing Income and Reputation.

Your Turn: Choose ONE of the following actions

🏗️ Place a Tile

Buy a tile from the market, place it adjacent to your city, then immediately resolve all of its effects.

🌊 Create a Lake

Purchase and place a Lake tile anywhere it is legally allowed.

🔄 2× Reactivate a Tile

Repurchase a tile already in your city and receive all of its effects again.

Finish Your Turn

Resolve Effects: Immediately apply every effect shown on the tile you placed or reactivated.
Population Track: Crossing a red line reduces both your Income and Reputation by 1.
End of Game: Add bonus points from the End of Game Goals to your final Population. Highest Population wins!
